## Why RTF Appears Short
RTF files are **compressed plain-text representations** of formatted documents. The same content that spans 69 lines in HTML appears in just 22 lines in RTF because:
1. **Forma1. **Forma1. **Forma1. ** RTF uses `\b` for bold, `\fs32` for font size, etc.
2. **No whitespace preservation** - HTML has visual spacing; RTF compresses it
3. **Display rendering** - When opened in Word/RTF viewer:
   - Content may appear on one "screen" initially
   - Scroll down or refresh to see all content
   - Different RTF viewers handle display differently
